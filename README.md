### R

- ## Heart Disease Risk Prediction using Statistical Modelling
  Analyzed clinical data from 918 observations to identify key drivers of heart disease. Developed and compared Generalized Linear Models (GLM) and Generalized Additive Models (GAM), evaluating performance through AIC, goodness-of-fit tests, and residual diagnostics. Designed robust preprocessing and imputation strategies while preventing data leakage.

- ## Wage Determinant Analysis
  Code: [Wage Determinant Analysis](https://github.com/AnnLe04/Projects/blob/main/Project.Rmd)
  Applied linear and logistic regression to Current Population Survey (CPS) data (534 observations) to uncover statistically significant wage disparities. Resolved severe multicollinearity (VIF > 5,000) using subset selection and stepwise AIC — the resulting model showed union membership increased the likelihood of higher wages by 3.27×.
